DID YOU KNOW?

Before you continue, discover a few remarkable facts about the civilization you just reconstructed.

01 · URBAN PLANNING

HARAPPA WAS A PLANNED URBAN WORLD

Mature Harappan settlements show organized layouts, streets and purposefully constructed urban spaces. At Harappa, the mature phase dates roughly to 2600–1900 BCE.

02 · WATER

WATER MANAGEMENT WAS A PRIORITY

Harappan cities developed sophisticated ways of managing water. Dholavira, for example, had reservoirs and systems for collecting, storing and directing water.

03 · TRADE

THEY WERE PART OF LONG-DISTANCE TRADE

Archaeological evidence from Dholavira shows inter-regional trade with other Harappan settlements and with regions including Mesopotamia and the Oman peninsula.

04 · CRAFT

CRAFTSMEN CREATED SPECIALIZED GOODS

Excavations at Dholavira have revealed workshops and objects made from materials including copper, shell, stone, terracotta, gold and semi-precious stones.

05 · A LONG HISTORY

HARAPPA WAS NOT BUILT IN A SINGLE ERA

The archaeological sequence at Harappa spans several periods, from early settlements through the mature Harappan phase and later phases. The site grew into a major urban centre.
