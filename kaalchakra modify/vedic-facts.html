DID YOU KNOW?

Before you continue, discover a few remarkable facts about the Vedic Age you just reconstructed.

01 · VEDIC TEXTS

THE VEDAS

The Vedas are among the oldest surviving sacred texts of India. They preserve hymns, prayers, rituals and ideas that provide important evidence about early Vedic society.

02 · SOCIETY

TRIBES AND COMMUNITIES

Early Vedic society was organised around families, clans and tribes. Communities were closely connected through kinship, shared rituals and pastoral life.

03 · AGRICULTURE

FROM PASTORAL LIFE TO FARMING

Cattle were highly important during the early Vedic period. Over time, agriculture became increasingly significant, especially as communities expanded towards the fertile Ganga plains.

04 · RITUAL & BELIEF

FIRE AND SACRIFICE

Rituals played an important role in Vedic religious life. Fire, known as Agni, held a central place in many ceremonies and was associated with offerings to the gods.

05 · LANGUAGE & CULTURE

THE LANGUAGE OF THE VEDAS

The Vedic hymns were composed in an early form of Sanskrit. Their oral transmission preserved the texts with remarkable attention to pronunciation and recitation.
